# 2026-09-09 New Website Address, everything else stays the same

Our online shops are currently moving from proxysto.re to **[proxystore.de](https://proxystore.de)**.

We had been planning this for a long time. Some visitors found the top-level domain ".re" confusing, while others didn’t understand the pun. We also occasionally had problems with overzealous spam filters. The final occasion to make the switch was a seven-hour outage of the old domain two days ago.

The old domain will remain active as a redirect. Nothing else will change.
